Session D: `generate_block2.py 585` mis-resolved footnote i (4346,
"Véase a, Mosiah 3; Mosiah 3:8; 4:2; 7:27; Helamán 16:18.") to the
wrong number and silently dropped the trailing literal citations,
producing "4346: Véase 1311." in librodm.txt. Root cause: the
script's VEASE_CLAUSE regex matches from "Véase " to the entry's
FIRST period, which for this entry is the very last character, so
the whole span (cross-reference plus the three trailing bare
citations) was fed to the single-clause resolver as if it were one
"Véase <letters>, <Book> <Chapter>" reference; its comma-based
book/chapter split then mis-parsed "Mosiah 3:8; 4:2; 7:27; Helamán"
as a book name and "16" as a chapter, and FootIndex.find_book_id()'s
fuzzy subset-match fallback let the garbled multi-word "book name"
match against the real, short "MOSIAH" book-header token set purely
because "MOSIAH" appears as one token inside the garbage — landing
on Mosíah chapter 16 letter a (1311, unrelated content) instead of
the intended Mosíah chapter 3 letter a. Manually corrected in
librodm.txt to "4346: Véase 1096; Mosiah 3:8; 4:2; 7:27; Helamán
16:18." (1096 confirmed as Mosíah 3a via librodm_foot.txt line 1268,
inside the LIBRO DE MOSIAH section spanning lines 1221-1835).
Footnote 4341 (d, "Véase s, I Nefi 13; Mormón 8:14; Moroni 10:1-2.")
hit the same mis-parse path (garbled "book name" fuzzy-matched I
Nefi, chapter mis-parsed as 10) but happened to stay correctly
unresolved only because I Nefi chapter 10 has no letter s in
librodm_foot.txt — not a safe outcome to rely on for a future page,
just lucky here. This script bug is not specific to this page (any
Block 1 entry combining a "Véase <letter>[, Book Chapter]" clause
with additional trailing bare citations, all under one un-terminated
sentence, is at risk) and should be fixed in generate_block2.py
itself rather than re-caught by hand on a future page; flagging for
the editor's attention rather than patching the script now.
